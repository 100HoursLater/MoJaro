# MoJaro
## Reserved/Restricted Keywords: 
-----------

### General Restricted:
- `import` & `from` 
- `fn` & `main()` 
- `class` & `struct`
- `enum`
- `public` & `private`
- `Property`
- `alloc` & `falloc`
- `sizeof`
- `return`
- `const`
- `self`
- `base`
- `if` & `else if` & `else`
- `for`

------
### Special Restricted:
- `int` 
- `float`
- `double`
- `bool`
- `string`
- `char`
-----------
